Urology-first, uro-oncology-depth navigator

Use the Oracle to derive a management plan from the entered clinical criteria. Use Calculators for scores/metrics only. The app intentionally summarizes guideline recommendations; it does not reproduce copyrighted guideline books verbatim.

Clinical safety: Drug doses and algorithms are reference protocols, not patient-specific prescriptions. Confirm current product label, renal/hepatic function, interactions, laboratory criteria, institutional chemotherapy order set, MDT decision and the latest live NCCN/EAU version before treatment.
